If you're working across multiple environments like Development, Test, or Production, identifying the current system just got easier! With **ABAP Platform 2023 / S/4HANA 2023 (Private Cloud Edition)**, SAP introduces a new **System Information Bar** in the Fiori Launchpad.

This bar appears just above the shell bar and displays key details such as the system role, tenant name, or client number — helping you instantly recognize the environment you're working in.

It's a simple yet powerful feature that boosts clarity, reduces errors, and improves the overall user experience.
